### How to read these figures

- Climate TRACE figures are **modelled estimates**, not metered measurements. They combine satellite observations, reported data and sector-specific models, and carry real uncertainty.
- Emissions are attributed to **where they are produced**, not where the resulting goods or energy are consumed. An area with a power station carries its emissions; the customers do not.
- Sub-national figures (states, counties, urban areas) are available from 2021 onwards; national and global figures from 2015. Earlier years return zero rather than an error.
- Many sub-national figures are **downscaled national totals** rather than independently observed. Where a source is marked as an administrative aggregation, its location is the middle of the area, not a facility.
