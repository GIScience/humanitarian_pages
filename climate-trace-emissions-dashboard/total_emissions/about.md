## About this indicator

Reports the greenhouse gases emitted within an area, broken down by sector, using the Climate TRACE
inventory. Climate TRACE combines satellite observations, reported data and sector-specific models to
estimate emissions worldwide — including for places that publish no inventory of their own.

### How to read it

Figures are modelled estimates, not meter readings, and are attributed to where emissions are produced
rather than where the energy or goods are consumed. Sub-national data begins in 2021. Because many
sub-national figures are downscaled from national totals, they are most useful for comparing the shape of
an area's emissions (which sectors dominate) rather than for auditing an exact tonnage.
