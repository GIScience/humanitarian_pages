## About Parking Density

Maps off- and on-street car parking — surface lots, multi-storey and underground garages and street-side parking — and measures how densely it is provided. A high parking supply is a signal of car-oriented urban design and of land given over to cars rather than to people.

### How to read these results

Car parks come from OpenStreetMap (amenity=parking), split by their parking=* type; individually mapped bays (amenity=parking_space) are an opt-in layer. Importantly, the indicator counts parking facilities, not spaces: a 500-bay garage counts the same as a three-bay lot, because capacity is inconsistently tagged. Results are a count and a density per km2, with a sub-area comparison and a heatmap. Enabling marked parking spaces alongside car-park polygons double-counts, because spaces are usually drawn inside a lot.
