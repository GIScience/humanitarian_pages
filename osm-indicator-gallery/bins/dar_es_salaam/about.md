## About Public Bins

Maps public litter bins and measures how well the area is served with places to throw away waste while out on foot. Good bin provision supports clean, pleasant and walkable streets.

### How to read these results

Litter bins come from OpenStreetMap (amenity=waste_basket); larger waste-disposal containers and dog-waste dispensers are opt-in layers. The indicator reports a count, a density per km2 and a walking-distance coverage view. Bins are patchily mapped in many places, so a low count may reflect missing data rather than missing bins — comparisons are fairest within one city.
