## About Organic Food Stores

Maps shops selling organic food, showing where residents can buy sustainably produced groceries close to home. It distinguishes fully organic shops from conventional shops that also stock an organic range.

### How to read these results

Shops come from OpenStreetMap. Organic is a property rather than a shop type: fully organic shops carry organic=only (a certified Bio-Laden), while organic=yes marks a conventional food shop with an organic range — the two are shown as separate categories. A bare shop=organic value is deprecated and not used. Because such shops scale with population, per-capita accessibility is the honest headline; a count, density and sub-area comparison are also provided, and opening hours feed an availability view.
