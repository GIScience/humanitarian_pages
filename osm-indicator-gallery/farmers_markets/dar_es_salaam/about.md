## About Markets & Farmers' Markets

Maps marketplaces where people can buy fresh, often local and seasonal food close to home — part of a shift toward more sustainable, lower-carbon food consumption.

### How to read these results

Markets come from OpenStreetMap (amenity=marketplace) via the ohsome API. An honest limitation: OpenStreetMap has no reliable tag that isolates farmers' markets, so this counts weekly, flea and farmers' markets alike. Opening hours, where present, encode the market days and feed an availability view. Because markets are sparse, per-capita accessibility (how many residents live within a short walk of one) reads more meaningfully than density per km2.
