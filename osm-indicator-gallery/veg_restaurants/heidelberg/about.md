## About Vegetarian & Vegan Restaurants

Maps vegetarian- and vegan-friendly restaurants, cafes and eateries — places offering lower-carbon, plant-forward food choices — and shows how accessible they are across the area.

### How to read these results

Eateries come from OpenStreetMap using the diet:* scheme over restaurants, fast food and cafes. Vegan-friendly and vegetarian-friendly places are shown as separate categories; because every vegan place is also vegetarian, the vegetarian category excludes the vegan ones to avoid double-counting. Places offering the options (diet = yes or only) are counted for density; the stricter fully-vegetarian/vegan case is too sparse to headline. As these amenities scale with population, per-capita accessibility is the honest metric, alongside a count, density and heatmap.
