## About EV Charging Stations

Maps electric-car charging stations and measures how densely they are provided across the area — a view of the infrastructure that supports decarbonising road transport.

### How to read these results

Stations come from OpenStreetMap (amenity=charging_station, keeping car-capable stations and dropping bike-only posts). The indicator counts stations, not connectors: a station's capacity (its number of charging points) is not summed, so a large multi-plug hub counts as one. An opt-in 'rapid / DC' layer identifies fast chargers by socket type, but that tagging is thin. Results are a count, a density per km2, a heatmap and a coverage view.
