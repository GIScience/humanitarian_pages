## About Mobility Points

Measures how close residents live to public transport and shared mobility — bus, tram, train, bike- and car-sharing points. It captures the walk-, bike-, bus- and train-ability of a neighbourhood for low-carbon everyday travel.

### How to read these results

Stops and stations come from OpenStreetMap in mutually exclusive categories (bus, tram/light-rail, heavy rail, opt-in metro, bike-sharing, car-sharing); overlapping bus tags are unioned so each stop counts once. The flagship output is a coverage view: the share of the area — and, where population data is configured, of residents — within a short walk of a mobility point. A count, density, category breakdown and heatmap round it out. Comparisons are fairest within one city, where stops are mapped with similar effort.
