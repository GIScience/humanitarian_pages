## About Audible Traffic Signals

Maps pedestrian traffic signals that provide acoustic guidance to help blind and partially sighted people cross safely. Like tactile paving, it is best read as a coverage indicator over signalised crossings.

### How to read these results

Signals come from OpenStreetMap using the traffic_signals:sound tag on signalised pedestrian crossings; broader 'any acoustic signal' and vibration-equipped layers are opt-in. This is a completeness ratio rather than a density: crossings without acoustic guidance are also mapped, so compare the equipped count against all signalised pedestrian crossings (in Heidelberg roughly two thirds carry acoustic guidance). Coverage depends heavily on local mapping effort, so comparisons are fairest within one city.
