## About Cool Locations

Maps and counts publicly accessible cool locations — shaded parks, cool church interiors, underground stations, public pools, libraries, museums, malls and water features — that offer relief during heat, and shows how densely they are distributed. It is a heat-resilience indicator for climate-change adaptation.

### How to read these results

Cool locations come from OpenStreetMap via the ohsome API, grouped into categories and each counted once. Results are a count, a density per km2, per-1,000-inhabitants where population data is configured, a category composition, a sub-area comparison, a heatmap and — for visitable venues with opening hours — an availability check for a hot Sunday afternoon. Explicitly private or closed features are excluded; untagged ones are assumed public. Density comparisons are fairest between neighbourhoods of the same city and least reliable across regions with different OpenStreetMap maturity.
