## About E-Bike Charging

Maps e-bike and pedelec charging points that support cycling as a low-carbon way to get around. It shows where riders can top up their battery across the area.

### How to read these results

Charging points come from OpenStreetMap (amenity=charging_station with bicycle=yes) via the ohsome API. Two caveats matter: the bicycle tag is set on only a minority of charging stations, so this undercounts, and bicycle=yes means 'bikes allowed' rather than 'bikes only', so some hits are combined car-and-bike posts. Results are a count, a density per km2 and a distance-based coverage view — best read as a lower bound given the tagging gaps.
