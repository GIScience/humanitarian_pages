## About Benches

Counts public benches and measures how many resting places the area offers per square kilometre and per resident. Benches support walkable, age-friendly neighbourhoods and give people — especially older or less mobile residents — somewhere to rest during hot weather.

### How to read these results

Benches come from OpenStreetMap (amenity=bench) via the ohsome API. The indicator reports a count, a density per km2 and, where population data is configured, benches per 1,000 inhabitants, plus a walking-distance coverage view of how much of the area is close to somewhere to sit. Results reflect OpenStreetMap completeness, which varies between places, so comparisons are most reliable within a single city where mapping effort is similar.
