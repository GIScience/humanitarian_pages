## About Private Pools

Maps and counts genuinely private, residential swimming pools across the area. Their density is a lens on individual heat-adaptation capacity — who can cool down at home — and, set against public cool locations, on a climate-justice dimension of heat resilience.

### How to read these results

Pools come from OpenStreetMap (leisure=swimming_pool) via the ohsome API, keeping only those without a public access value — a bare swimming-pool tag is overwhelmingly private garden pools. Results are a count, a density per km2, per-1,000-inhabitants where population data is configured, a sub-area comparison and a heatmap. Because private pools are mapped unevenly, density comparisons are fairest between neighbourhoods of the same city rather than across regions.
