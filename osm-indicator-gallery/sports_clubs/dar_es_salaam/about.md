## About Sports Clubs & Centres

Maps sports clubs and sports centres to show how well a neighbourhood is served with places to be physically active. Nearby, walkable sport and leisure options are part of a liveable, low-car neighbourhood.

### How to read these results

Data comes from OpenStreetMap: sports clubs (the organisation, club=sport) and sports centres (the facility, leisure=sports_centre); commercial gyms (leisure=fitness_centre) are an opt-in layer. Because clubs are only sparsely mapped as such, the density signal leans on sports centres. Results are a count and a density per km2, with a category breakdown and a sub-area comparison; opening hours, where present, feed an availability view. Completeness varies between places, so compare within a city.
