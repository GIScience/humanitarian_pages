## About Swimming Pools

Maps swimming pools in the area and separates publicly accessible bathing sites from private garden pools. Public pools are important heat refuges, and the split between public and private pools is a lens on who can cool down and how.

### How to read these results

Pools come from OpenStreetMap (leisure=swimming_pool), classified by access: public and community pools (plus water parks and public baths) versus private/residential pools. The access split matters because a bare swimming-pool tag is overwhelmingly private garden pools. Note that some municipal pools are mapped as sports centres with sport=swimming and are not captured here. Results are a count, a density per km2, a public/private composition and a heatmap; opening hours feed an availability view for the public pools.
