## About Waste Water Infrastructure

Maps the visible parts of local waste-water infrastructure — sewage treatment plants, stormwater and sewage basins, and sewage pumping stations. It provides a quick inventory of where these facilities are, supporting sanitation and water-management planning.

### How to read these results

Facilities come from OpenStreetMap via the ohsome API. Treatment plants use the canonical man_made=wastewater_plant; basins, sewage-typed storage tanks and pumping stations are separate categories (pumping stations must be qualified as sewage/wastewater so clean-water pumps are not counted). Because treatment plants and pumps are very sparse — often only a handful per city — read the result as a count/inventory rather than a fine per-km2 density. Much of the sewer network is underground and unmapped, so absence on the map does not mean absence on the ground.
